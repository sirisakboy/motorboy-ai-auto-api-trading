PROMPT B — GENERATE THE CHARACTER ARTWORK (image models)
================================================================

Works with Midjourney, DALL·E, Imagen, Flux, Stable Diffusion.
Use B1 for a single hero render, B2 for a consistent multi-pose set.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
B1 — SINGLE HERO RENDER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Full-body 3D stylized character mascot, friendly young man, short spiky black
hair, light stubble, confident warm smile, arms crossed.
Outfit: black leather bomber jacket with dark-red zipper trim and a small red
"M" logo on the chest, black t-shirt, black cargo pants, black lace-up boots,
black wristwatch.
Pixar-like soft shading, clean studio lighting, high detail on the leather
texture.
Transparent background (PNG alpha), centered, full body from head to boots,
front view, neutral stance, no shadow under the feet, no text, no watermark,
no props.
Style reference: modern app mascot / 3D character key art, 4k.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
B2 — CONSISTENT 6-POSE SET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Same character, same outfit, same lighting, 6 separate full-body renders on a
transparent background:
1) idle, smiling, arms crossed
2) pointing forward as if explaining
3) thumbs up with a confident grin
4) thinking, hand on chin
5) surprised / warning expression, hands raised
6) tired, shoulders dropped

Keep proportions, palette and camera identical across all six. Front view, no
text, no watermark, transparent PNG, full body head to boots.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
OPTIONAL — UI EXPRESSION HEADSHOTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Same character, head-and-shoulders bust, expressive face only, 6 emotions
(neutral, happy, thinking, surprised, concerned, tired), identical lighting and
framing, transparent PNG, no text. Use these as status-dot avatars or toast
icons at 32-64px.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
IF THE MODEL CANNOT DO ALPHA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ask for a flat, evenly lit solid background (solid #00FF00 or solid white),
no cast shadow, no gradient, and cut the background out afterwards. Never let
the render include a floor shadow — it breaks the floating overlay look.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
NEGATIVE PROMPT (append for SD / Flux style tools)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
text, watermark, logo overlays other than the chest mark, extra limbs,
extra fingers, deformed hands, harsh rim light, busy background, floor shadow,
low resolution, blurry, cropped feet, photorealistic skin pores, neon outline
